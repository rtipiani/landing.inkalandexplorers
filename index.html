Frequently Asked Questions

What is the best time to visit Machu Picchu?

The dry season (May-October) is generally the best time, with clear skies ideal for photography. However, Machu Picchu is stunning year-round.

Do your packages include flights?

Our packages cover everything from arrival in Cusco: hotel, meals, tours, transfers, and entry tickets. International flights are not included but we can help you plan them.

How do I deal with altitude sickness?

We recommend arriving in Cusco a day early to acclimatize. We provide guidance on altitude, and our guides are trained to assist if you feel unwell.

Can I customize my itinerary?

Absolutely! All packages can be tailored to your needs. Contact us on WhatsApp and we'll build the perfect trip for you.

What is your cancellation policy?

We offer flexible cancellation up to 30 days before departure with a full refund. See our terms for full details.
